title: Quick start
description: Open Vango inside Nimiq Pay and earn your first stamp.
---

Vango is a mini app. It runs inside the Nimiq Pay wallet you already have on your phone,
so there is nothing to install and no account to create. Your wallet is your identity and
your stamp card at the same time.

## Open it

Tap this link on the phone that has Nimiq Pay installed:

```text
https://nimpay.app/miniapps/open/vango.app
```

Nimiq Pay opens Vango in its own window. You can also reach it from the mini app list
inside Pay, or by pasting the address into Pay's **Custom URL** field, which is how you
open a development build (see [Get test NIM](/docs/getting-started/get-test-nim)).

## What happens on the first open

1. Vango asks your wallet who is here. Pay answers with your two addresses and Vango shows
   the first one, shortened, at the top of the screen.
2. If your wallet is still catching up with the chain, Vango says **wallet syncing** and
   waits. That lasts a few seconds after Pay starts. It is not an error.
3. Vango asks the server for a login message and hands it to your wallet to sign.

## The one signature

You sign exactly once, at login. The message looks like this:

```text
vango-login:4f2c9ab1e5d0742c8b1a6f3e9c0d5817:1789119232753
```

It is a random string plus the minute it stops being valid. There is no money in it and no
permission attached to it. Signing it proves you hold the key to your address, which is all
the login needs. The message works once and expires in ten minutes, so a copy of it is
worth nothing to anybody.

Tap **Cancel** in that dialog and nothing breaks. Vango stays on the welcome screen and
offers the button again.

## Your first stamp

1. Open a shop's card, from its link or by scanning the QR on the counter.
2. Vango shows what the card gives you, what one visit costs, and where the money goes: the
   shop's own wallet address, never Vango's.
3. Tap **Pay and stamp**. Pay shows its normal send dialog with the amount and a memo like
   `vango:5FQQ2J56` already filled in. Confirm it.
4. The payment lands on the Nimiq chain in about a second. Vango reads it back from the
   chain and your card shows **1 of 5**.

Nimiq fees are zero, so the only NIM that leaves your wallet is the payment to the shop.
